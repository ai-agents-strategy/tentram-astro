# B2B Strategy for Tentram Cleaning Service

## Target Segments

### 1. Property Management Companies 🏢
**Why:** They manage multiple properties and need regular cleaning services
**Approach:** Offer bulk packages for all properties under management
**Value Prop:** Consistent quality, flexible scheduling, one invoice for all properties

**Companies to approach:**
- Jendela360 (already in pipeline)
- Apartemen360
- Bookingkost.com
- My Property Indonesia
- PT Usaha Gedung Mandiri

### 2. Real Estate Developers 🏗️
**Why:** Need post-construction cleaning for new properties
**Approach:** Offer packages for new handover cleaning
**Value Prop:** Specialized after-renovation cleaning, quick turnaround

**Companies to approach:**
- WIKA Realty
- Waskita Realty
- NWP Property
- Binakarya Propertindo
- PT Adhi Commuter Properti

### 3. Hotels & Resorts 🏨
**Why:** Need regular housekeeping and deep cleaning
**Approach:** Offer monthly/weekly cleaning packages
**Value Prop:** Professional housekeeping, 24/7 availability, quality guarantee

**Companies to approach:**
- Novus Jiva Villa Resort & Spa
- Novotel Lombok Resort & Villas
- Rajavilla Lombok Resort
- Puri Bagus Villa Resorts
- Sapphire Sky Hotel BSD

### 4. Airbnb / Short-Term Rental Hosts 🏠
**Why:** Need turnover cleaning between guests
**Approach:** Offer per-cleaning or monthly packages
**Value Prop:** Quick turnaround, checklist-based, 5-star cleaning

**Companies to approach:**
- Omah Begalon Homestay
- Individual Airbnb hosts (via social media)

### 5. Coworking Spaces & Offices 🖥️
**Why:** Need daily/weekly cleaning for shared spaces
**Approach:** Offer daily cleaning contracts
**Value Prop:** Flexible scheduling, professional service, minimal disruption

**Companies to approach:**
- Coworking Virtual Office
- Dojo Bali Coworking
- Terracassa Coworking Space Semarang

### 6. Retail & Shopping Malls 🛒
**Why:** Need daily cleaning for large spaces
**Approach:** Offer cleaning contracts for common areas
**Value Prop:** Large team availability, specialized equipment

**Companies to approach:**
- NWP Property (shopping malls)
- Local shopping centers

### 7. Cleaning Service Companies (Partnership) 🧹
**Why:** Can refer overflow work or specialize in different areas
**Approach:** Offer partnership or subcontractor arrangement
**Value Prop:** Reliable partner, quality guarantee

**Companies to approach:**
- Sirman Cleaning Service
- Hagios Cleaning
- Bali Villa Cleaning

### 8. Schools & Universities 🎓
**Why:** Need regular cleaning for large facilities
**Approach:** Offer semester or annual contracts
**Value Prop:** Experience with educational facilities, flexible scheduling

### 9. Hospitals & Clinics 🏥
**Why:** Need specialized cleaning with strict hygiene standards
**Approach:** Offer specialized medical facility cleaning
**Value Prop:** Trained staff, hospital-grade cleaning products

### 10. Restaurants & Cafes 🍽️
**Why:** Need kitchen deep cleaning and regular maintenance
**Approach:** Offer kitchen deep cleaning packages
**Value Prop:** Food safety compliance, grease removal specialists

## B2B Outreach Strategy

### Phase 1: Research (Week 1-2)
1. Identify 50 target companies across all segments
2. Research each company's cleaning needs
3. Find decision-makers (Facility Manager, Operations Manager, GM)
4. Prepare personalized outreach messages

### Phase 2: Outreach (Week 3-4)
1. Send personalized emails/WhatsApp messages
2. Offer free consultation or trial cleaning
3. Follow up within 3-5 days
4. Track responses in CRM

### Phase 3: Conversion (Week 5-8)
1. Conduct site visits for interested companies
2. Provide custom quotes
3. Negotiate terms
4. Close deals

### Phase 4: Retention (Ongoing)
1. Regular check-ins with existing clients
2. Quality assurance surveys
3. Referral programs
4. Annual contract renewals

## B2B Pricing Strategy

### Volume Discounts
- 5-10 properties: 10% discount
- 11-20 properties: 15% discount
- 21+ properties: 20% discount

### Contract Terms
- Monthly contracts: Standard pricing
- Quarterly contracts: 5% discount
- Annual contracts: 10% discount

### Special Packages
- **New Property Handover Package:** Deep cleaning + sanitization
- **Turnover Cleaning Package:** Quick clean between tenants/guests
- **Maintenance Package:** Weekly/bi-weekly regular cleaning
