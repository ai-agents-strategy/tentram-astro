# Apartment Management Strategy for Tentram Cleaning Service

## Market Opportunity

### The Problem
Many apartment owners in Indonesia want to convert their properties into **passive income** but face challenges:
- ❌ No time to manage tenants
- ❌ No experience in property management
- ❌ Difficulty finding reliable tenants
- ❌ Maintenance and cleaning issues
- ❌ Vacancy periods = lost income

### The Solution
**Tentram Apartment Management Service** - A complete package that includes:
1. **Occupancy Management** - Find and retain tenants
2. **Housekeeping Services** - Regular cleaning and maintenance
3. **Passive Income** - Owner receives monthly income without hassle

## Risk Mitigation

### Common Risks
1. **Tenant Damage:** Document condition, require deposit
2. **Vacancy:** Marketing strategy, competitive pricing
3. **Service Quality:** Regular training, quality checks
4. **Payment Issues:** Clear contracts, late fees

### Mitigation Strategies
1. **Insurance:** Property insurance for damage
2. **Contracts:** Clear terms and conditions
3. **Quality Control:** Regular inspections
4. **Communication:** Regular updates to owners
